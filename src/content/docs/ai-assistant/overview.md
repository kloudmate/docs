# AI Assistant overview

## Overview

The KloudMate AI Assistant lets you query your logs, metrics, and traces using natural language. Instead of writing complex queries, you can ask questions like "Why is the payment service slow?" or "Show me errors from the past hour".

## What the AI Assistant can do

- Translate natural language questions into log or metric queries
- Summarise recent incidents and anomalies
- Suggest alert rules based on observed patterns
- Explain error messages and stack traces

## How it works

The AI Assistant uses your connected observability data as context. It does not store your data or send it to third-party AI providers beyond what is needed to generate a response.

## Prerequisites

- At least one data source connected
- AI Assistant feature enabled for your workspace (available on Pro and Enterprise plans)
