---
title: "Slack Integration"
description: "Documentation for Slack Integration"
---
The Incident Management Settings allows you to connect a Slack channel to the Incident Management module, enabling your team to receive and manage incidents directly from Slack.

Step 1: Go to **Incidents**>**Settings** from the left navigation menu.

![image](./images/slack-integration-1.png)

Step 2: Under the Chat Ops tab, click **Connect** on the Slack integration window.

Step 3: You will be redirected to Slack’s authorization page. Select the Slack channel you want to connect using the dropdown, add a message for your App Manager, and click Submit.

<hint type="info">
  Once the workspace is connected, each team member will need to link their personal Slack account to manage incidents from Slack.
</hint>
