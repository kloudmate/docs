An **incident** refers to any event or issue that may negatively impact your services, application workflows, end users, or customers.

KloudMate automatically detects issues within your connected services, letting you address them before they impact your applications. All such issues are tightly integrated with a robust Incident Management module, enabling your team to manage them efficiently once they start causing disruption.

KloudMate’s **Incident Management module** is designed to help you streamline your end-to-end incident response strategies and workflows. Your teams can detect, delegate, notify, track, and collaborate to resolve incidents, all within a single, unified platform.

## Key Components in Incident Management 
![image](./images/what-is-incident-management_-1.jpeg)

### Overview

The Overview screen provides a real-time snapshot of all incidents in your infrastructure, displaying key stats such as Triggered, Acknowledged, Resolved, and Total incident counts, along with MTTA and MTTR metrics. 

To learn more, see the [Incident Management Overview](/platform/incident-management/) documentation.

### Incidents

An incident is any event or issue that may negatively impact your services, application workflow, or customers. 

To learn more, see the [Incidents](/platform/incident-management/incidents/) documentation.

### Services

Services within KloudMate’s Incident Management module enable you to group incidents based on the service or microservice they belong to. 

To learn more, see the [Services](/platform/incident-management/services/) documentation.

### Integrations

Integrations are webhook endpoints that can be called from external services to send incidents into KloudMate. You can create multiple integrations for each service. 

To learn more, see the [Integrations](/platform/incident-management/integrations/) documentation.

### Escalation Policies

Escalation policies define the escalation steps for incidents. For each step, you can configure how and who to notify if an incident remains unacknowledged or unaddressed. 

To learn more, see the [Escalation Policy](/platform/incident-management/escalation-policy/) documentation.
