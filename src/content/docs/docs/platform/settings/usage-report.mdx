The usage report shows usage and cost across all workspaces in your organization for the selected period.

It includes:

- **Usage by type:** the amount used, the rate, and the cost for each type you used: logs, traces, metrics, voice calls, workflow runs, log rehydration, RUM sessions, session replays, and profiles, with the total cost for the period
- **Usage breakdown:** pick a usage type and split it by workspace, source, source type, service, app, workflow, or feature. A chart shows the largest groups, and a table lists every group with its share and its change from the previous period. Owners and billing admins can also split AI credits by person.

![image](./images/usage-report-1.png)

Use the period selector to switch between quick ranges (the last 24 hours, 7 days, or 30 days), the current billing period, and previous billing periods.

![image](./images/usage-report-2.png)

## Custom Date Range Reports

To report on any other window, select **Custom range…** from the period selector, then enter a start time and an end time in `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm` format.
