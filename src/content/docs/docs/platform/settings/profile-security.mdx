Use **Profile & Security** to manage your account details and authentication settings.

## Available Actions

- Change your display name
- Update your password
- Enable or disable multi-factor authentication (MFA)

## Update Your Profile

1. Open **Settings -> Profile & Security**.
2. Update your name or password in the relevant fields.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

## Enable or Disable MFA

1. Open **Settings -> Profile & Security**.
2. Turn on MFA from the authentication section.
3. Register the account with your authenticator app.
4. Store the MFA recovery secret safely.

On future sign-ins, you will be prompted for the MFA code generated by the authenticator app.

To disable MFA, return to the same page and use the **Disable MFA** action.
