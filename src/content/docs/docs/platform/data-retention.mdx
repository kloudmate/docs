How long KloudMate keeps your data depends on what it is. Logs and traces are kept for the window your plan sets, metrics for a year, and several other kinds of data have their own window.

This page lists every retention window, including the ones you can't see in the product.

## Plan retention

Logs and traces are kept for the window your plan sets. This is the data you can query directly in the Log Explorer and the trace views.

| Data | Retention |
| --- | --- |
| Logs and traces | Free 7 days, Pro 14 days |
| Metrics | 1 year |

Metrics are billed by sample count rather than by how long they're kept, so they're kept for a year on every plan.

Logs and traces retention is set by your plan. See [Subscription & Billing](../settings/subscription-billing/).

## Metrics are tiered by resolution

A metric isn't kept at full resolution for the whole year. Raw samples are kept for about 3 hours, then served from rollups at coarser resolution: a 15-day tier, a 63-day tier, and a long rollup kept for the full year. KloudMate picks the resolution for the range you query, so a chart over the last hour reads raw samples and a chart over the last quarter reads the long rollup.

## Other retention windows

These apply to everyone, regardless of plan.

| Data | Kept for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session replay recordings | 7 days | The rrweb recordings behind a RUM session. Not archivable to Cold Storage. |
| Continuous profiling | 7 days | |
| Anomaly forecast bands | 7 days | The predicted band a forecast alert compares against. |
| Issue occurrences | 30 days | |
| Metric cardinality analytics | 45 days | |
| Endpoint (RED) history | 90 days | An hourly rollup, kept so request-rate, error, and duration history outlives raw trace retention. |
| RUM route metrics | 90 days | An hourly rollup of per-route performance. |
| RUM custom events | 400 days | An hourly rollup, so you can compare this quarter against last quarter. |
| RUM release health | 400 days | An hourly rollup, so you can compare a release against an earlier one after their raw sessions have aged out. |

Raw RUM data follows your plan retention. That covers sessions, errors, journeys, raw events, route visits, and the raw custom events behind the 400-day rollup.

## Keeping logs longer

To keep logs beyond your plan retention, archive them to your own S3 bucket with [Cold Storage](../settings/cold-storage/). The archive lives in your AWS account at S3 storage prices, and you rehydrate a time range into the Log Explorer when you need to search it. Cold Storage archives logs only. Traces, metrics, and RUM aren't archived.
