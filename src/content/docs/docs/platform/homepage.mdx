The KloudMate homepage is the first screen you see after signing in. It gives you a consolidated view of service health, recent issues, alarms, and usage trends so you can understand the current state of your environment at a glance.

## What the Homepage Shows

The homepage brings together high-level platform signals such as:

- Service health and activity
- Recent issues and alarms
- Cost and usage insights
- Key observability widgets selected by your team

## Customize the Homepage

### Enter Edit Mode

Click **Edit** in the top-right corner of the homepage to start customizing the layout.

### Move and Resize Widgets

While in edit mode, you can:

- Drag and drop widgets to change their position
- Resize widgets to fit the layout you want

### Add a Widget

Click **Add Widget** to open the widget panel and choose from the available pre-built widgets.

### Remove a Widget

Each widget includes a delete action in its header. Remove widgets that are not useful for your team or role.

### Save or Undo Changes

- Click **Save** to keep your updated layout
- Click **Undo** to discard recent changes before saving

## Adjust the Time Range

Use the time range selector in the top-right corner of the homepage to update the time window across all homepage widgets at once.
