The Incident Management Overview screen provides a bird’s-eye view of all incidents within your infrastructure. It gives you access to all important incident stats and your assigned incidents in one place.

To navigate to the Overview screen, click **Incidents** in the left navigation menu, then select **Overview**.



## Incident Statuses

An incident within KloudMate goes through three status indicators within its lifecycle:

- **Triggered — **Any issue declared as an incident starts in the Triggered state, indicating that it needs to be reviewed by the relevant team.
- **Acknowledged —** This status indicates that the incident has reached the relevant team and is being actively reviewed.
- **Resolved —** This status indicates that the incident has been fixed and closed by the relevant team.

At the top of the screen, you will see key stats across all incidents, the **total count** of incidents in each status, **MTTA** (Mean Time to Acknowledge), and **MTTR** (Mean Time to Resolve).

Below the stats, the **My Incidents** list displays the incidents assigned to you, along with relevant details such as title, severity, triggered time, responders, and current status. Click **View All** to see the complete list of incidents across your workspace.
