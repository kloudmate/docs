Use the following steps to create a new service.

To create a new service, click the **Create Service** button at the top right corner of the Services screen.

![image](./images/creating-services-1.jpeg)

**Step 1: **Enter the **Service Name** and **Description**.

**Step 2:** Select a **Service Owner** from the dropdown to assign a team member responsible for managing the service and its incidents.

**Step 3:** Select an **Escalation Policy** for the service, or Create new escalation policy. This will serve as the default policy for all incidents under this service. 

<hint type="info">
  Escalation policy is mandatory for a service. If no escalation policy exists while creating a service, you are required to create a new one before proceeding.
</hint>

**Step 4: **Set the **SLA Target** percentage for the service.

![image](./images/creating-services-1.png)

Once all details are filled in, click **Save**. The service will appear in the Services list.
