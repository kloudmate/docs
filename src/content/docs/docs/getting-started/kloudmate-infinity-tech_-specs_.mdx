**KloudMate Infinity** is a managed observability solution, that deploys effortlessly on customer infrastructure, requiring minimal setup.

Depending on requirements, there are **2 options** to deploy KloudMate Infinity on your infrastructure:

- On Linux VMs, or
- A Kubernetes environment (EKS, AKS, GKE,etc... or self-hosted)

<hint type="info">
  Note, that **KloudMate Infinity** differs from our regular SaaS offering.

  Sign up for the KloudMate SaaS offering [here](https://app.kloudmate.com/signup).
</hint>
