Open any incident from the incident list to view its detail page.

![image](./images/incident-details-1.png)

The header shows:

- Assigned responders
- Related service
- Source integration
- Applied escalation policy

You can also add or remove responders from this screen.

## Incident Metrics

The detail page shows key incident fields such as:

- **Status**
- **Severity**
- **Suppressed Count**
- **Occurrence Count**
- **Triggered At**
- **Acknowledged At**
- **Resolved At**
- **Time to Resolve**

## Incident Summary

The summary section contains the incident description and a link to the related alarm for deeper investigation.

## Activity Tabs

At the bottom of the page, you can review:

- **Notes:** Team notes and investigation context
- **Logs:** A timeline of assignments, acknowledgements, and other actions
- **Alarms:** The alarms attached to the incident

![image](./images/incident-details-2.png)

Use **Acknowledge ** or ** Resolve** from the top-right corner to update the incident status.
