Open any incident from the incident list to view its detail page.

![image](./images/incident-details-1.png)

The header shows:

- Assigned responders
- Related service
- Source integration
- Applied escalation policy

You can also add or remove responders from this screen.

## Incident Metrics

The detail page shows key incident fields such as:

- **Status**
- **Severity**
- **Suppressed Count**
- **Occurrence Count**
- **Triggered At**
- **Acknowledged At**
- **Resolved At**
- **Time to Resolve**

## Incident Summary

The summary section contains the incident description and a link to the related alert for deeper investigation.

## Linked SLOs

When the affected service has at least one incident-based [SLO](../../../reliability/), a **Linked SLOs** panel appears below the standard service / integration / escalation-policy labels. Each linked SLO shows as a card with its name, kind + target + window, and current compliance %. Clicking a card opens the [SLO detail](../../../reliability/slo-detail/) page.

The panel is **only rendered when at least one incident-based SLO exists on the affected service** — incidents on services without reliability tracking show no panel.

## Activity Tabs

At the bottom of the page, you can review:

- **Notes:** Team notes and investigation context
- **Logs:** A timeline of assignments, acknowledgements, and other actions
- **Alerts:** The alerts attached to the incident

![image](./images/incident-details-2.png)

Use **Acknowledge** or **Resolve** from the top-right corner to update the incident status.
