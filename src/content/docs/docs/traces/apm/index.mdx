Application Performance Monitoring (APM) is KloudMate's service-centric experience for understanding latency, throughput, error rate, and service dependencies.

It is built from tracing data and RED-style operational metrics. Use APM when you want to compare services, inspect dependency edges, or move quickly from an unhealthy service to the traces and logs behind it.

## What APM Covers

- service health and request volume
- latency percentiles such as P99, P95, and P50
- error rate and throughput trends
- dependency relationships between services
- quick navigation into related traces, logs, issues, and dashboards

APM surfaces request-rate, error-rate, and latency views at the service level. Broader application, infrastructure, database, and custom metric documentation lives under [Metrics](../../metrics/).
