## Configuring an Email Notification Channel

**Step 1:** Select**Email** from the dropdown menu.

![image](./images/email-1.png)

**Step 2:** Enter a**Name**for the channel (e.g., demo) and your email address in the Address field.

**Step 3:**  Click**Submit**.
