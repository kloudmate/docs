KloudMate provides powerful visualization tools to help you monitor, analyze, and extract insights from your telemetry data. Whether you need a persistent view of critical infrastructure KPIs or an ad-hoc workspace to troubleshoot an ongoing incident, KloudMate's visualization suite has you covered.

This section covers the two primary ways to visualize your data:

## In This Section

- **[Dashboards](./dashboards/)**
  Build persistent, customizable views of your most important metrics. Dashboards allow you to combine multiple visualization panels—such as time series charts, bar charts, lists, and statistical summaries—into a single pane of glass. You can create them from scratch or use pre-built templates for common use cases like Kubernetes or database monitoring.

- **[Explore](./explore/)**
  Use the Explore workspace for ad-hoc querying and validation. Explore allows you to query metrics, logs, and traces on the fly, apply complex aggregations, and derive insights before optionally saving those queries directly into a persistent Dashboard panel.
