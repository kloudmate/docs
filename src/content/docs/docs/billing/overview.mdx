KloudMate offers usage-based pricing with monthly subscription plans. This page explains how billing works and where to manage your subscription.

## Plans

| Plan | Description |
|---|---|
| Free | Up to 3 users, 7-day data retention, limited integrations |
| Pro | Unlimited users, 30-day retention, all integrations, AI Assistant |
| Enterprise | Custom retention, on-premises option, SSO, dedicated support |

## How usage is calculated

Billing is based on the volume of data ingested per month:

- **Logs** — charged per GB ingested
- **Metrics** — charged per million data points ingested
- **Traces** — charged per million spans ingested

Exact pricing is shown in your workspace under **Settings → Billing**.
