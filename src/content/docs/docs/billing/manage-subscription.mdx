This guide explains how to view, change, or cancel your KloudMate subscription from the workspace settings.

## Prerequisites

- Workspace Owner role

## Steps

### View current plan

Go to **Settings → Billing**. Your current plan, renewal date, and next invoice amount are displayed here.

### Upgrade your plan

Click **Upgrade plan** and select the plan you want. Your new plan takes effect immediately and is prorated for the current billing period.

### Downgrade your plan

Click **Change plan** and select a lower tier. Downgraded plans take effect at the start of the next billing period.

### Update payment method

Click **Update payment method** to change your credit card or billing details.

### Cancel your subscription

Click **Cancel subscription**. Your workspace remains active until the end of the current billing period, then moves to the Free plan.

## Troubleshooting

If your subscription change does not take effect, contact [support@kloudmate.com](mailto:support@kloudmate.com) with your workspace ID.
